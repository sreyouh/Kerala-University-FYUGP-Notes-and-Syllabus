Kerala University FYUGP BA — Syllabus, Notes & Study Materials

This page provides free access to the Kerala University FYUGP BA syllabus for all disciplines and semesters. Whether you are looking for the FYUGP Economics syllabus, the FYUGP English syllabus, the FYUGP Malayalam syllabus, or the FYUGP Political Science syllabus — everything is organised semester-wise for easy access.

The revised FYUGP BA syllabus under the Four Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUGP) follows the National Education Policy 2020 framework adopted by Kerala University. Students can download syllabus PDFs, access curated notes, question banks and previous year question papers for each semester from Semester 1 through Semester 6.

Economics

The FYUGP BA Economics syllabus covers microeconomics, macroeconomics, Indian economy, and quantitative methods across six semesters. Notes and question papers are available semester-wise.

English

The FYUGP English syllabus includes British literature, American literature, Indian writing in English, linguistics and literary theory. The revised syllabus follows Kerala University FYUGP guidelines.

Malayalam

The FYUGP Malayalam syllabus covers classical poetry, modern literature, linguistics, grammar and folk literature. Access semester-wise notes and the revised Malayalam syllabus PDF here.

Political Science

The FYUGP Political Science syllabus covers political theory, Indian government, international relations and public administration. Semester-wise notes and the revised syllabus are available to download.
