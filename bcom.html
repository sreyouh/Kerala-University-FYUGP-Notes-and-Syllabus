About FYUGP BCom

The FYUGP BCom programme at Kerala University covers core commerce disciplines including Financial Accounting, Corporate Accounting, Business Law, Cost Accounting, Income Tax, Auditing, Financial Management and Marketing Management across six semesters. The revised BCom syllabus under FYUGP integrates multiple exit options — Certificate after Semester 2, Diploma after Semester 4, and the full Bachelor of Commerce degree after Semester 6, in line with the NEP 2020 framework.

Students looking for FYUGP BCom notes download, BCom question papers Kerala University, or the revised BCom syllabus can find all available materials on this page. New resources are added regularly as the portal grows.
