Kerala University FYUGP BSc — Syllabus, Notes & Study Materials

This page provides free access to the Kerala University FYUGP BSc syllabus for all science disciplines and semesters. Whether you are looking for the FYUGP Chemistry syllabus, the FYUGP Physics syllabus, the FYUGP Mathematics syllabus, the FYUGP Botany syllabus, the FYUGP Zoology syllabus, or the FYUGP Computer Science syllabus — everything is organised semester-wise for easy access and download.

The revised FYUGP BSc syllabus under the Four Year Undergraduate Programme follows the National Education Policy 2020 framework adopted by Kerala University. Students can access curated notes, syllabus PDFs, question banks and previous year question papers for each semester from Semester 1 through Semester 6 across all science disciplines.

Chemistry

The FYUGP BSc Chemistry syllabus covers organic, inorganic and physical chemistry across six semesters. The revised syllabus includes renewable energy chemistry, spectroscopy and analytical techniques. Notes and question papers are available semester-wise.

Physics

The FYUGP Physics syllabus includes mechanics, electrodynamics, optics, quantum mechanics and semiconductor physics. The revised FYUGP Physics syllabus follows Kerala University NEP 2020 guidelines with updated course content.

Mathematics

The FYUGP Maths syllabus covers calculus, algebra, differential equations, real analysis and numerical methods. Access the revised FYUGP Mathematics syllabus and semester-wise notes and question papers here.

Botany

The FYUGP Botany syllabus covers plant diversity, cell biology, plant physiology, ecology and economic botany. Download the revised FYUGP Botany syllabus and access semester notes directly from the discipline tab above.

Zoology

The FYUGP Zoology syllabus covers animal diversity, genetics, ecology, cell biology and developmental biology. The revised Zoology syllabus under Kerala University FYUGP is available semester-wise on this page.

Computer Science

The FYUGP Computer Science syllabus covers programming, data structures, algorithms, database systems and software engineering. Access the revised FYUGP CS syllabus and semester-wise study materials here.
