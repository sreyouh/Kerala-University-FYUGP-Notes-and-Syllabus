This Privacy Policy applies to the website sreeharis.in and all pages hosted under it, including the Kerala University FYUGP Notes Portal and Espace Systems (sreeharis.in/espace). It is operated by Sreehari S, an individual based in Kerala, India.

By accessing or using any part of this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the site.

This policy is drafted in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA). It also adheres to Google's Publisher Policies.

2. Scope of This Policy

This single, comprehensive policy covers two distinct services under sreeharis.in, each with different data practices. Please read the section relevant to the service you are using.

  • sreeharis.in (FYUGP Notes Portal) — Educational resource platform for Kerala University students. No personal data is collected here.
  • Espace Systems (sreeharis.in/espace) — A web design and digital solutions service. Limited personal data is collected solely for appointment scheduling.

Section A
sreeharis.in — FYUGP Notes & Science Hub

Data Collection

This website does not collect any personal data from its visitors. You can browse all notes, syllabi, question papers, and Science Hub experiments completely anonymously.

Specifically, we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, IP address (for identification), or any other information that could identify you personally. There are no user accounts, registration forms, or login requirements on this site.

Cookies & Tracking

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to sreeharis.in.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

We have no control over Google's data practices. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Third-Party Links

This portal may contain links to external websites (such as university official pages or Google Drive for note downloads). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before sharing any information with them.

Educational Purpose

All content on sreeharis.in — including notes, syllabi, question papers, and Science Hub experiments — is provided purely for educational purposes for Kerala University FYUGP students. Content is not used to collect data or profile users in any way.

Espace Systems — Web Design & Digital Solutions

What Data We Collect

Full Name To address you correctly Used in our response and scheduling communication
Phone Number or Email Address To contact you for your consultation Sent via WhatsApp to confirm and conduct the consultation

⚠️ We do not collect sensitive personal data as defined under Indian law — including financial information, health data, passwords, or government IDs.

How We Collect It

Data is submitted voluntarily by you through the booking form. When you click "Confirm Slot Booking", your information is sent directly via WhatsApp to Sreehari S (the founder of Espace Systems). No data is stored in a database or on our servers — the submission opens your WhatsApp application with a pre-filled message.

Purpose of Collection

The information you provide is used solely and exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To schedule and confirm your free consultation appointment
  • To contact you at your preferred date and time
  • To discuss your web design or digital services requirements
  • To provide you with a cost estimate or proposal if requested

We will not use your data for any marketing, newsletters, or promotional communications without your explicit consent.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), we process your data based on your informed consent, which you provide when you voluntarily submit the consultation form. By submitting the form, you agree to the use of your data as described in this policy.

Data Retention

Since no database is maintained, the data you submit exists only within the WhatsApp conversation initiated by your form submission. This conversation is retained for the duration of our business interaction and for a reasonable period thereafter for reference purposes, typically no longer than 12 months after our last interaction, unless you request earlier deletion.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with any third party for commercial purposes. Your data is seen only by Sreehari S (founder, Espace Systems) and is used solely for the consultation you requested.

We may disclose your information only if required to do so by law, court order, or a competent governmental authority under Indian law.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. These include:

  • The website is served over HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption) to protect data in transit
  • Form submissions are sent directly through WhatsApp's encrypted messaging infrastructure
  • No personal data is stored on unsecured servers or databases by Espace Systems

While we strive to protect your information, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights Under Indian Law

Under the DPDPA, 2023, as a data principal (user), you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent to processing at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the legality of processing done before withdrawal.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer (details below) or with the Data Protection Board of India once established.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer at the details provided below. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request, as required under Indian law.
